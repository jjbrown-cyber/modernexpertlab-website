01

Why do AI, Google and LinkedIn favour real experts?

AI tools, Google and LinkedIn want sources they can trust. They look for credible and verifiable professionals with proven experience and a clear opinion. Your LinkedIn profile and posts show who you are and prove what you know.

02

Why do company websites struggle to appear in AI answers?

AI tools trust what others say about you more than what you say about yourself. A real, named expert sharing what they know carries more weight than a carefully polished company claim like “we’re the best in the industry”. That trust improves your AI discoverability.

03

How does expert visibility drive sales?

People find your experts on LinkedIn and in AI tools while they're still trying to understand their problem. They ask questions. Trust starts early, and that trust leads to sales.
