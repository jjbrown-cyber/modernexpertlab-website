Privacy policy

Last updated 7 October 2026

This policy explains how The Modern Expert Lab collects and uses your personal data when you visit this website, contact me, book a call, join my newsletter or work with me. I follow UK data protection law, including the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

Who is responsible for your data?

My name is Justyna Brownbridge. I run The Modern Expert Lab from York, United Kingdom, and I am the data controller for the personal data described in this policy.

You can contact me at justyna@modernexpertlab.com.

What personal data do I collect?

  • Enquiry form. Your name, company email, company, budget, how you heard about me and your question.
  • Call bookings. Your name, email and any notes you add when you book a call through Calendly or HubSpot.
  • Newsletter. Your first name and email address if you subscribe on my website. My newsletter is also published on LinkedIn, where LinkedIn manages your subscription under its own privacy policy.
  • Clients. Contact and billing details, and the information you share during workshops, coaching and programmes, such as LinkedIn profiles and draft posts.
  • Online community. Your profile and messages if you join my community on Skool.

I don't ask for sensitive personal data, such as health information. Please don't include it in messages.

Why do I use your data, and on what legal basis?

What I doLegal basis under UK GDPR
Reply to your enquiry or questionLegitimate interests, to respond to people who contact me

I never sell your data, and I don't use it for automated decisions about you.

Who do I share your data with?

I use trusted service providers to run my business. They only process your data on my instructions.

  • Website domain and hosting through Namecheap
  • Website analytics through Google Analytics and HubSpot
  • Enquiry forms, newsletter sign-ups and contact records through HubSpot
  • Call bookings through Calendly and HubSpot
  • Email, calendar and video calls through Google Workspace
  • Newsletter through LinkedIn
  • Online community through Skool
  • My accountant, for financial records

Some of these providers store data outside the UK, for example in the United States. When that happens, I rely on safeguards approved under UK law, such as the UK Extension to the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or the UK International Data Transfer Agreement.

How long do I keep your data?

  • Enquiries that don't lead to work. Up to 2 years after our last contact.
  • Client records and invoices. 6 years after the end of the tax year, as UK tax law requires.
  • Newsletter subscribers. Until you unsubscribe. Every email has an unsubscribe link.

Does this website use cookies?

This website uses Google Analytics to understand how visitors use it, for example which pages people read and how they found the site. Google Analytics sets cookies and collects information such as your device, browser, approximate location and the pages you visit. It doesn't tell me who you are. Google processes this data on my behalf and may store it outside the UK. The website also loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may record your IP address. This website doesn't use advertising cookies.

HubSpot also sets cookies to count visits and see which pages people read. If you fill in a form on this website, HubSpot can link your earlier visits to the details you gave me. HubSpot processes this data on my behalf and stores it in the EU.

Google Analytics and HubSpot cookies are only set if you click “Accept” in the cookie banner. You can change your choice at any time.

What are your rights?

Under UK GDPR you have the right to

  • ask for a copy of your personal data
  • ask me to correct data that is wrong
  • ask me to delete your data
  • ask me to limit how I use your data
  • object to how I use your data
  • ask me to move your data to another provider
  • withdraw your consent at any time, for example by unsubscribing from the newsletter

To use any of these rights, email me at justyna@modernexpertlab.com. I'll reply within one month.

How can you make a complaint?

If you're unhappy with how I use your data, please contact me first so I can put it right. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint or on 0303 123 1113.

Will this policy change?

I may update this policy when my services or the law change. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
