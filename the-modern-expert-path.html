1. Define your expertise
Core

2. Find what matters
Curator

3. Join the conversation
Contributor

Comment on others' posts, and share chosen content with your own view.

4. Share your own ideas
Creator

Plan and write your own posts and opinions.

5. Lead your field
Catalyst

Lead change within the industry and inspire others.
